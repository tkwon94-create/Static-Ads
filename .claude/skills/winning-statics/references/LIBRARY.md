# The Reference Library — 9 Families, 50 Winners

Every entry is a real static captured from a live ad library, stored exactly as captured in this folder (`ref-NN-slug.png`). Family depth varies — Big-Claim Product Hero is the deepest (13); Editorial/Advertorial is the thinnest at two references — be honest about that when a user asks for a large single-family batch.

Per-reference notes below are the recreation-critical details: what carries the persuasion, what the QC pass must check, and which protocols fire.

---

## Family 1 — Photographic Before/After (3 refs)

Real-photo transformation splits and timelines. Persuasion mechanism: **credibility through unretouched realism** — visible texture, real lighting, real people. The imperfection IS the proof. Realism protocol: ON (enforce natural skin texture, protect the 'before' from beautification, lock identity across panels). Strongest possible version: drop in the user's own real customer photos and let the model handle only typography and framing.

## Family 2 — Illustrated Before/After & Mechanism Diagrams (6 refs)

Drawn or 3D-rendered condition contrasts and how-it-works anatomy. These sidestep every photo-credibility problem because the style is openly illustrative — the viewer files it as education, not evidence. Mechanism: **self-diagnosis** ("that is what's happening inside me"). Realism protocol: OFF — stylization is the point.

## Family 4 — Us-vs-Them Comparison (6 refs)

Two-column tables, side-by-sides, ranked tests, 'ordinary vs ours' framings. Mechanism: **choice architecture** — you define the criteria, so your product wins. Targets solution-aware and most-aware buyers, swaps cleanly across niches, almost no realism risk. One rule enforced on recreation: **competitor trademarks get genericized** — layout replicated, other brands' marks are not.
